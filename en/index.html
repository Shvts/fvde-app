FAQ

Where are my photos sent?

Nowhere. All processing happens on your phone; photos never leave the device.

Where are finished frames saved?

To Photos on your phone. With “Save to the fvde album” on, they also go into a separate album.

The app can’t see my photos or camera

Open iOS Settings → fvde and allow access to Photos and Camera.

I don’t get the “Your film is developed” notification

Make sure notifications are on for fvde: iOS Settings → fvde → Notifications.

How do I turn off statistics or news?

In fvde: Settings → “Help improve fvde” or “fvde news”.

“My shots” takes up a lot of space

In fvde: Settings → Clear. Shots already saved to Photos and disposable-camera rolls stay.
