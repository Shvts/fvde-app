Privacy Policy

Updated October 28, 2026

fvde is an iPhone app that turns your photos into film frames. This page explains, briefly and honestly, what data the app uses, where it goes, and how to turn it off.

The short version: your photos never leave your phone. All processing happens on the device. There are no ads, no tracking, and we never sell data.

Photos and camera

  • Camera access lets you shoot in fvde; Photos access lets you pick a picture and save the result. The app uses neither without your permission.
  • Photos are processed only on your phone and are never sent to us or anyone else.
  • “My shots” and disposable-camera rolls are stored inside the app on your phone. If iCloud Backup is on, they are included in it like any app’s data; we have no access to your iCloud.
  • Clear “My shots” in fvde under Settings → Clear. Delete the app and everything on the phone goes with it.

Crash reports

If the app crashes or hits an error, it sends a technical report to Sentry (USA): where in the code the error happened, the phone model, iOS version and app version, plus a note that the app was launched, so we can tell what share of launches go without a crash. If the error happened while opening a photo, the report may include the technical name of a temporary file — a random string of letters and numbers; the photo itself is never sent. Reports contain no photos and nothing about you. We use them to find and fix bugs.

Usage statistics

To learn which features and films people use, the app sends anonymous events to PostHog (EU), such as “film selected” or “camera button tapped”. Each event includes the app version, phone language and a random number the app creates itself. It isn’t linked to your name, Apple ID or advertising identifier. No photos, locations, file names or personal details are sent. Like any website, the service sees your connection address (IP), but the app asks it not to work out your country or city from it.

To turn it off: Settings → Help improve fvde. Nothing is sent after that.

fvde news

If you turn on “fvde news” in Settings (off by default), we’ll occasionally send notifications about new films and features. For this, our database (Supabase, EU) stores the notification address issued by Apple, your phone language, how the app was installed (TestFlight or App Store) and the app version. Notifications are delivered via Expo and Apple. Turn the switch off and the address is deleted.

“Your film is developed” notification

This notification is scheduled by the phone itself. Nothing is sent anywhere for it.

App updates

On launch, the app asks Expo whether an update is available. Only technical details are sent: the app version, the update channel and current update number, and the project ID.

What we don’t do

  • show ads or use advertising identifiers;
  • track you across other apps or websites;
  • sell data or share it for advertising;
  • ask you to sign up or collect your name, email or phone number.

Children

fvde is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information about them.

Your rights

You can turn off statistics and news in Settings at any time, or delete the app — everything on the phone goes with it. To ask what data we hold or to request deletion, email fvde.app@gmail.com.

Changes

If the app starts using data differently (for example, if purchases are added), we’ll update this page and the date above.
